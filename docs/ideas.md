# PortfolioPilot design brief

## Design Movement
A quiet editorial WealthTech interface: Swiss modernist precision softened by warm paper, generous spacing and data made legible. It should feel like a thoughtful private-client briefing, not a trading terminal.

## Core Principles
1. **Evidence before excitement:** lead with actual or clearly marked sample numbers and explain every score, assumption and comparison.
2. **Calm guidance:** use neutral language, no FOMO, price predictions, promises or trade calls.
3. **Progressive clarity:** put the key conclusion first, reveal methodology and detail nearby, and keep complex analytics understandable.
4. **Inclusive utility:** keyboard-accessible controls, legible contrast, responsive layouts and explicit loading, empty, error and success states.

## Color Philosophy
Ink navy (`#15263B`) conveys steadiness; a restrained blue (`#55799A`) signals analysis and navigation. Warm off-white (`#F5F7F7`) reduces glare and makes the app feel human. Teal (`#2B7A6D`) is reserved for positive movement, amber (`#A76D25`) for attention and muted red (`#B6544D`) for negative impact. Color never carries meaning alone; pair it with labels and symbols. The ownable PortfolioPilot signature color is **Harbor Blue** (`#55799A`).

## Layout Paradigm
A purposeful split between compact persistent navigation and an editorial work canvas. Desktop uses a quiet left rail and broad content area with selected insights adjacent to primary charts; landing content uses asymmetric text-and-visual panels rather than a centered grid. Mobile converts navigation to a compact bottom bar, stacks content in reading order and lets data tables scroll horizontally.

## Signature Elements
- A compass-like “P” mark formed from a portfolio path and a single north-pointing tick.
- Fine baseline rules and small numbered section markers that make analysis feel like a considered briefing.
- A thin path motif repeated in portfolio-history and goal-projection charts, with colored endpoint labels rather than decorative gradients.

## Interaction Philosophy
Make the next useful action obvious without urgency. Save and analysis actions confirm their actual status; editable assumptions update calculations immediately and visibly; irreversible account deletion requires explicit confirmation. Use tooltips for definitions, labels for all fields, visible focus, chart tables/legends and clear retry actions.

## Animation
Use brief 140–200 ms opacity/position transitions for navigation, drawers and cards; animate chart changes only when data changes. No looping or parallax. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`, and never delay financial results for an animation.

## Typography System
Use a humanist sans stack (`Inter`, `Aptos`, `Segoe UI`, system-ui) for navigation and body copy; use tabular numerals for INR values, dates and percentage metrics. Headings are sentence case, compact and moderate-weight; explanatory text stays comfortably readable at 14–16 px. Do not fetch remote fonts.

## Brand Essence
**Positioning:** PortfolioPilot helps Indian retail investors connect portfolio choices to real-life goals with clear analytics and illustrative stress scenarios—without a broker login or trade execution. **Personality:** considered, clear, dependable.

## Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and human; CTAs describe an action, not a promise. Microcopy explains what is known, what is assumed and what happens next. Examples: “Know what your portfolio is carrying.” “See how an illustrative shock could affect your goals.”

## Wordmark & Logo
A custom geometric “P” combines two offset portfolio bars with a fine path arcing to a compass point; the wordmark uses a purposeful letter-spaced sans treatment. Keep the symbol recognizable at favicon size and avoid stock/coin imagery.
